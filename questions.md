# Question bank

Starter set. Add real questions from interviews and from the internship as you go.

## Career change (you will be asked this every time)
- Tell me about your transition from music to cloud security.
- Why should we trust a self-taught engineer with production infrastructure?
- What did performing on cruise ships teach you that applies to this job?

## Behavioral (answer in STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result)
- Tell me about a time something you built failed. What did you do?
- Describe a disagreement with a co-founder or teammate and how you resolved it.
- Tell me about a time you had to learn something fast under a deadline.

## Cloud security scenarios
- An S3 bucket in production is found publicly readable. Walk me through your response.
- How would you let GitHub Actions deploy to AWS without long-lived keys?
- A developer needs temporary admin access to debug production. How do you handle it?
- How do you detect and prevent drift between Terraform and what is actually deployed?
